description=[h1]Zombie Skins: Expanded[/h1]

description=

description=[h2]Changes From the Original[/h2]

description=[list]

description=[*][b]Shove immunity added.[/b] These zombies can no longer be knocked down just by walking up and pushing them. Weapon crits still work as intended.

description=[*][b]Optional rain/snow/fog-only spawning.[/b] New sandbox toggle, off by default.

description=[*][b]Optional night-only spawning.[/b] One toggle for every skin, plus one per skin, limited to 12am-6am game time. It combines with the rain/snow/fog option instead of replacing it: turn on both if you want!

description=[*][b]Night sprinters and a configurable night window.[/b] Pick the night hours (e.g. 10pm-5am, wrapping past midnight is fine). Optionally, a random share of these zombies sprints during the window, and an optional spawn multiplier scales how many appear. Both are re-rolled every night between a min and max you set, with a chance of calm nights and optional moon-phase scaling. All on the new Night sandbox page, off by default.
description=[*][b]Optional fog-only spawning.[/b] Separate from the rain/snow toggle, with an adjustable fog density required (default leans heavy). Combines with the night options.
description=[*][b]Higher health pools, and they stay damaged.[/b] The per-skin health cap is raised from 30 to 500 (very high values are untested), and health is now set once per zombie instead of being reset every few seconds, so damage no longer heals back.
description=[*][b]Civil Defense Sirens compatibility.[/b] If you run Civil Defense Sirens, when a siren that attracts zombies goes off, a few of these zombies come out of nearby buildings for each player who can hear it. Nothing to configure, and nothing happens without that mod.
description=[*][b]Exploder blast radius fixed.[/b] It wasn't actually circular — zombies in the corners of the blast zone were taking damage from farther away than the radius said they should.

description=[*][b]Two ultra-rare alien skins.[/b] A Grey Alien and Skinny Bob: sprinters and climbers that scream in their own alien voices, drawing zombies toward them. They are roughly 50x rarer than any other skin by default, and by default they only appear between 12am and 6am game time. Every setting, including that one, is adjustable in the sandbox under the Aliens page. Models, textures and voice clips are from [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3759103750]Alien Events[/url] by [url=https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198109648219]TheS1X[/url] (see Credits).

description=[*][b]PhunSprinters 2 compatibility.[/b] If you run a setup w. PhunSprinters2 that controls when vanilla zombies sprint by time of day, these skins won't cause issues.

description=[*][b]Compatibility with Bandits2[/b] 

description=[*][b]Project A-Life compatibility.[/b]

description=[*][b]They See You compatibility.[/b]

description=

description=

description=[h2]Sandbox Options[/h2]

description=[list]

description=[*]Per-skin Enable / Spawn Chance / Health

description=[*][b]Shove Knockdown Immunity[/b] — these zombies can't be shoved to the ground. Weapon crits still drop them.

description=[*][b]Spawn Only During Precipitation or Fog[/b] — optional, restricts all 23 types to rain, snow, or fog

description=[*][b]Spawn Only At Night[/b] — optional, for all skins or per skin (12am-6am game time by default, window adjustable). Works alongside the precipitation option

description=[*][b]Alien Voices[/b] — the aliens scream with their own voice clips (on by default)

description=[*][b]Screamer Skins Ignore They See You[/b] — on by default, no effect if you don't run that mod

description=[/list]

description=

description=[h2]Zombie Types[/h2]

description=[list]

description=[*][b]Runners[/b] — sprint instead of shambling

description=[*][b]Climbers[/b] — can scale walls and obstacles

description=[*][b]Wall Breakers[/b] — smash through fences, barricades, doors, and windows

description=[*][b]Toxic[/b] — poison nearby players on a cooldown (hazmat suits protect you)

description=[*][b]Screamers[/b] — shriek and pull in nearby zombies

description=[*][b]Exploders[/b] — detonate on death, damaging nearby zomboids(configurable damage and and option to emit fire on blast)

description=[/list]
